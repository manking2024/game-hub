Take a 4x4 jeep over rocky mountains, muddy trails and steep hills: balance on uneven ground, crawl over boulders and reach the destination on extreme off-road tracks.

How do you control the jeep?

W or Up Arrow drives forward, S or Down Arrow reverses, A/D steers left and right. On rough ground the key is momentum, not speed: keep just enough throttle over rocks and dips so the jeep doesn't flip or get stuck.

What terrains will you cross?

Rugged mountains, rocky trails, muddy paths and steep hills all show up across the levels. Each surface changes how the jeep grips, so you re-learn your line on every new track.
