Play Retro Snake Classic Online

Retro Snake Classic brings back the arcade favorite: steer your snake, eat the food, grow longer — hit a wall or yourself and it's game over. One second to learn, hard to put down.

What's new in this classic?

Beyond the original mode, speed now ramps up as your snake grows, so late-game pacing gets noticeably faster. To chase a high score, deliberately leave room to maneuver early on — don't let your own tail trap you against the wall.

How do keyboard and touch controls work?

On desktop, use the arrow keys or WASD to steer. On mobile, swipe on the screen in the direction you want to go. Both control schemes respond instantly, so sharp turns are never a problem.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What's the highest possible score in Snake?

    There's no fixed cap — it depends on how long you survive and how long your snake gets. Once the speed climbs, margin for error shrinks; most players finish somewhere between 200 and 500 points.

  • What if the game loads slowly?

    It's a lightweight HTML5 build — normally 1–2 seconds on a decent connection. If it ever seems stuck, just refresh the page and it will reload.
