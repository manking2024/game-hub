Play Geometry Vibes X Ball Online

Geometry Vibes X Ball is a fast one-button arcade hopper: click and hold to fly up, release to dive, and thread through obstacles, traps and gears across new levels and a challenge mode.

How do you control it?

It is a true one-button game. Hold the left mouse button or press and hold the Up Arrow to fly upward, release to dive down. Everything — timing a jump, clearing a gap, squeezing between spinning gears — comes down to when you hold and when you let go. That single input is the whole skill ceiling.

In 2-4 player modes, the extra players use the Up, H and L keys on the same keyboard, so a group can race without any controllers or accounts.

How is X-Ball different from the original Geometry Vibes?

The original campaign is the pure one-button obstacle run. X-Ball keeps that core and layers three additions on top: new interactive levels, a dedicated challenge section and a completely rebuilt store. If you already know the first game, the new levels and the store make X-Ball feel fresh instead of a re-skin. For a full feature comparison with the 3D edition, check the version you are playing.

What should a new player do in the first 10 minutes?

  • Play Endless for three runs. Feel the hold/release rhythm without worrying about failing.
  • Watch the gears, not the ball. Obstacles come at you in predictable lanes; your eyes should track the gap you are flying into.
  • Try one Challenge level. It forces precise edge control and teaches you how tight the hitbox really is.
  • Open the store and read prices. Understand what currency is for before you spend a single coin.
  • Race a friend once. Multiplayer is where the game gets loud — and where the controls prove themselves.

Can I play with friends on one device?

Yes. X-Ball supports 1-4 player modes on the same keyboard. Player 1 uses the mouse or Up Arrow; players 2-4 use Up, H and L. No controllers, no accounts, no network setup.
