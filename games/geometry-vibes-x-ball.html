Play Geometry Vibes X Ball Online

Geometry Vibes X Ball is a fast one-button arcade hopper: click and hold to fly up, release to dive, and thread through obstacles, traps and gears across new levels and a challenge mode.

Game provided by a third-party platform. If it fails to load, refresh the page.

How do you control it?

It is a true one-button game: hold the left mouse button or press and hold the Up Arrow to fly upward, release to dive down. Everything — timing jumps, clearing gaps, threading between gears — comes down to when you hold and when you let go. In 2-4 player modes, other players use Up, H and L keys.

What is new in the X-Ball mode?

Beyond the original campaign, X-Ball adds interactive new levels, a dedicated challenge section and a completely rebuilt store, so runs feel fresh even if you already know the first game. Each level still boils down to dodging obstacles, traps and gears.
