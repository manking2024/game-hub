How do you play Chubby Finn?

Chubby Finn is a grid-based puzzle where you steer a chubby seal around with the arrow buttons. Your goal is to push every ball onto its glowing target position. The catch: a ball can only be pushed, never pulled, so one careless shove can block a level forever. Before you start pushing, walk the path in your head — plan every turn so Finn never traps a ball or himself against the wall.

What makes it worth playing?

Levels are designed as bite-sized brain teasers — short enough for a coffee break, tricky enough to make you rethink your first move. The art stays cute and clean, and it runs smoothly on both desktop and touch screens, so you can pick it up on any device without an install or a sign-up.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is Chubby Finn free?

    Yes. The game is completely free to play in your browser, supported by non-intrusive ads.

  • How do I control Finn?

    On desktop, use the on-screen arrow buttons or your keyboard's arrow keys. On mobile, tap the arrow buttons shown on the game board.

  • What if I push a ball the wrong way?

    Balls can't be pulled back, so if you trap one, simply restart the level. Each level is short, so a reset costs only a few seconds.
