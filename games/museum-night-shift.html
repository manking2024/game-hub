How do you solve it?

Museum Night Shift is a turn-based puzzle game where every step changes the plans of a cunning thief. Patrol a hex-grid museum, block escape routes and use switches to open or close security gates. Catch the thief before the emerald is stolen, or chase him down and recover it. Solve 50 handcrafted nights across five museum wings, earn coins for efficient captures and complete your exhibit album. Finish the campaign to unlock New Game+ with double coin rewards. No timer: take your time and plan the perfect interception.
