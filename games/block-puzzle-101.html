Block Puzzle 101 is a free HTML5 brain teaser: drag blocks onto a 10x10 board and fill full rows or columns to clear them. Rounds last 3–5 minutes — perfect for commutes and short breaks.

How do you play Block Puzzle 101?

The game hands you blocks in random shapes, one at a time. Drag each one onto the 10x10 board; whenever a row or column is completely filled, it clears and scores points. The game ends when the board has no room for the next block. The core strategy is to keep space open for long I-shaped blocks — resist the urge to fill every corner too early.

Why play it in your browser?

The web version needs no installation and takes up zero phone storage. On desktop it plays precisely with a mouse or trackpad, and on mobile you drag with your finger. Nothing to update, nothing to sign up for — just open the page and start a round.
