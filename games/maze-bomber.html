Play Maze Bomber Online

Fight your way through a maze of bombs and blocks: place explosives strategically, blast open paths, dodge your own blasts, and find the route to the exit on every level.

How do you play Maze Bomber?

Move through the maze and drop bombs next to destructible blocks. The blast opens a path, but the same explosion can trap you or knock you out, so leave yourself an escape route before every placement. Read the layout, find the best route and break through to the goal.

How hard is it?

Early levels teach you the rhythm of placing and dodging; later mazes stack more blocks, tighter corridors and trickier placement choices. It rewards thinking one step ahead more than fast reflexes.
