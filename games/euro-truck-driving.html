Play Euro Truck Driving Online

Sit in the cab of a European truck and haul cargo across scenic roads: finish delivery missions, stay on the route, and feel what long-haul driving is like without the paperwork.

Game provided by a third-party platform. If it fails to load, refresh the page.

How do you drive the truck?

W or Up Arrow accelerates, S or Down Arrow brakes and reverses, A/D steers. A truck brakes later and turns wider than a car, so the main skill is planning your turns early and not slamming the brakes.

What are the missions like?

You haul valuable cargo between points on realistic roads. Every journey adds a new challenge — tighter routes, busier traffic and longer runs — while the environments give you that long-haul feel.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is Euro Truck Driving free?

    Yes, free to play in your browser with no sign-up.

  • Does it work on mobile?

    Yes, touch controls are supported for on-screen driving.

  • What if I crash the cargo?

    You can reset and retry the mission — treat it as a learning lap.
