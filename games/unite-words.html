Play Unite Words Online

Find groups of four related words and put them in one row: swap words between rows, complete sets before the timer runs out, and use hints when you get stuck.

Game provided by a third-party platform. If it fails to load, refresh the page.

How do you play?

Rows hold scrambled words. Pick four that belong together — a category, a synonym set, a themed group — and place them in one row to complete the set. Swap words between rows to shuffle the layout. Special sets, once finished, reveal new words to group.

What happens if you get stuck?

You can call a hint to highlight a correct group, and the clock is your pressure. Levels are quick enough that it works as a vocabulary warm-up or a short break.

How do word sets work?

Unite Words gives you a grid of tiles and asks you to find groups of four related words and line them up in one row. You swap words between rows and complete each set before the timer runs out. When you get stuck, the hint system nudges you toward the next connection.
