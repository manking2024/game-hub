Play Demolition Tycoon 3D Online

Start with a mini loader and build a demolition fleet: hold to smash buildings, earn coins, upgrade power and speed, and unlock seven machines across 80 contracts in seven districts.

Game provided by a third-party platform. If it fails to load, refresh the page.

How do you demolish?

Hold the demolition button (or Space) to strike a building. Every hit chips it apart and pays coins, which you pump back into power, speed, profit and critical hits before saving for bigger machines. Wood, brick, concrete, metal and glass all behave differently under impact.

What is the progression?

You start at a backyard and work through seven districts — up to a harbor and alpine heights — across 80 contracts, unlocking seven machines including the Titan crusher. After the campaign, tougher contracts keep the loop going.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is Demolition Tycoon 3D free?

    Yes, free to play in your browser with progress saved locally.

  • How many machines can I unlock?

    Seven machines, including the heavy Titan crusher.

  • What controls does it use?

    Mouse, touch or keyboard — hold to strike, Esc opens settings.
