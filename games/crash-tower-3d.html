Play Crash Tower 3D Online

A physics destruction game: launch a vehicle, hit the boost in the gold zone, and smash through 30 increasingly tough walls, towers and houses. Earn coins, unlock vehicles and upgrade them.

Game provided by a third-party platform. If it fails to load, refresh the page.

How do you launch and boost?

Click or tap Start Engine to launch. The boost marker sweeps back and forth — press Space or tap boost exactly when it crosses the gold zone for maximum impact. Timing the boost is what turns a bump into a full demolish.

What do the coins buy?

Every impact earns coins you spend in the garage on three unlockable vehicles and separate upgrades for each one. If a structure survives, repair the damaged vehicle and try again. The destruction yard also shifts from daylight to sunset and night.
