Play City Delivery Rider Simulator Online

Ride through a busy city as a delivery courier on a motorcycle: follow checkpoints, drop parcels before the timer runs out, dodge traffic and manage fuel across increasingly tough routes.

Game provided by a third-party platform. If it fails to load, refresh the page.

How do you drive?

Press W or the Up Arrow to accelerate, S or Down Arrow to reverse, steer with A/D or the Left/Right Arrows. Space brakes, Shift shifts your weight, C changes the camera and R resets the bike. Touch controls work on mobile, so you can play one-handed on a phone.

What makes runs tense?

Each route gives you a checklist of customers and a clock. You have to read the traffic, plan turns and manage fuel, because running out of gas or missing a checkpoint fails the delivery. Later routes stack tighter timers and denser streets.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is City Delivery Rider free?

    Yes — free to play in your browser, supported by ads.

  • Does it work on a phone?

    Yes, touch controls are built in, so you can steer on the road with on-screen buttons.

  • What if I crash?

    The bike resets with R, so you can recover quickly and keep the delivery going.
