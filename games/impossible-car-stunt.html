Race over ramps and across impossible tracks: launch off jumps, pull stunts, avoid traps and reach the finish line on increasingly wild stunt courses.

How do you drive?

W or Up Arrow moves forward, S or Down Arrow reverses, A/D or Left/Right Arrows steer. The trick is speed control: jump too fast and you overshoot the ramp, too slow and you stall mid-air. Use the mouse to tap any on-screen button when you need precision.

What are the tracks like?

Each level is a fresh stunt adventure — narrow sky ramps, sudden gaps, rotating obstacles and traps that punish sloppy landings. Clearing a track cleanly feels like a small victory.

    Most levels let you reset or retry, so a bad landing just means another run.
